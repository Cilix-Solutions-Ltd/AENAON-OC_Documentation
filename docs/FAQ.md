# Frequently asked questions

[Repository home](../README.md) · [Getting started](GETTING_STARTED.md) · [Tool catalogue](TOOL_CATALOGUE.md)

## Must I use all AENAON tools?

No. Select one or several tools according to your approved use case and agree the scope through onboarding.

## How do I access a hosted tool?

Ask the responsible provider through your project onboarding contact, or [info@aenaon-project.eu](mailto:info@aenaon-project.eu) for routing. The provider confirms eligibility, workspace, roles and credentials. Repository access does not grant service access.

## Where are the installation instructions?

Read the tool profile first. Hosted services require onboarding rather than beneficiary installation. Where deployment is authorised, the owner supplies applicable prerequisites, resources and instructions through onboarding.

## Can I download the software?

Only if its owner explicitly supplies and authorises the relevant artefacts. A catalogue entry or Docker-based architecture does not imply a public download. Proprietary hosted services exclude source-code and self-managed deployment access as described in their profiles.

## Can I use my own data?

Agree the permitted inputs and handling conditions with the provider. You must have permission to share and process the information. Never place credentials or sensitive operational data in the repository.

## What if production data cannot be shared?

Discuss sanitised, synthetic or representative inputs with the provider before onboarding the dataset. Do not upload production data to work around access constraints.

## Are APIs available?

Availability and scopes depend on the tool and authorised activity. CTIP and SCTIP describe controlled REST/JSON access; other profiles identify REST interfaces or conditional API arrangements where supplied. Obtain actual endpoints, documentation and authorisation from the owner. Repository access does not enable an API.

## Can I deploy a service on my infrastructure?

The Digital Twin describes Docker-based on-premises or private-cloud deployment subject to ZELUS supplying authorised resources. ASAT describes a Docker application stack, with arrangements confirmed by ZELUS. The cyber range uses provisioned environments. Do not assume unrestricted deployment. CILIX, PRIVACT and ENDOSCOPE hosted services do not include self-managed deployment.

## Where can I find additional documentation?

Follow approved resources on each tool's profile or request the relevant manuals through the provider's onboarding route. The [xBOMGuard guide](../tools/xbomguard/README.md) contains browser onboarding and optional authorised API workflows.

## Who maintains each tool's information?

ZELUS maintains its three profiles, CILIX its two, PRIVACT its two and ENDOSCOPE its two. Providers propose changes to their own material through pull requests. CILIX maintains common documentation, has final approval authority and merges approved updates. Beneficiaries read the approved contents; they do not push or merge changes. See [contribution instructions](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
